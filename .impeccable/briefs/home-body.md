# Home

Mode: Persuade

Audience: someone in or near Clovis deciding on a haircut, a time, and which barber to book.

Action: leave for that barber’s Square book. The on-site pages exist so the choice is informed.

Direction: category-standard barbershop site, chosen to sit beside Ruffians. Near-black field, Bodoni Moda, antique gold, one book action. Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/comp-a-bleed.jpg`.

The hero background is the shop’s own video, `public/video/hero.mp4`, with `public/video/hero-poster.jpg` as the still. It is a daylight storefront. A left-weighted scrim keeps the name and button readable without burying the sign.

Memorable moment: the real front of the shop moving behind the name, then the three books.

Do not literalize the comp’s stock fade, the label “THE TIME” as a kicker, or invent a promotional price. Faces in the comp are not the barbers.
