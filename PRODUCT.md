## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Astro on Cloudflare Pages. The user left the stack open and required a Cloudflare deploy. Astro is the fit because the site is a marketing landing page plus one indexable page per barber, with no app backend.

## Users

A person in or near Clovis, California who is considering a haircut and needs to see what the shop offers, how long a service takes, and how to book a specific barber. That includes adults, parents booking for kids, and anyone who wants to know they will be welcomed before they walk in.

## Product Purpose

A public website for Prolific Cuts at 493 W Shaw Ave, Ste A, Clovis, CA 93612. It sends visitors to the shop’s real booking platforms and to an individual page for each barber who currently books under the shop. Success is a visitor who understands the services and durations, picks a barber, and leaves for that barber’s booking page.

## Positioning

Prolific Cuts is a shared shop where each barber keeps their own book. The site’s job is to make that easy to see: one shop, separate barbers, each with a public menu of services and times, and a room that is meant to be welcoming to every kind of person who needs a cut.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive comparing shops, often on a phone, and need an address, hours, a service with a duration, and a booking link. Booking happens on Booksy, not on this site. Walk-ins happen at the shop; public reviews say appointments are the safer plan when the room is busy. Each barber’s bookable hours live on their own Booksy profile and do not match a single shop-wide day.

## Product Principles

- Send people to a real barber’s book. The site does not pretend to take the appointment.
- Show a service with its time. A price without a duration, or a duration without saying whose menu it is, fails the visit.
- Welcome is a fact about how the room treats people, shown through who can book and what parents already say, not through a slogan stack.
- Missing facts stay visibly missing.
- One shop, separate books. Never collapse three menus into a single invented price list.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The shop’s public listings describe a wheelchair-accessible entrance, parking, and restroom. The client asked the site to make clear that the experience is welcoming to every kind of person who needs a cut, including children and people who are new to a barbershop. The site itself must be usable with a keyboard, readable type, and text that is not carried by color alone.
